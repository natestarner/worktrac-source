3. Free and Pro

Free is free, permanently, and is not a trial. It includes unlimited workouts, sets, exercises and people, offline logging, personal records, routines, the rest timer, and a full export of your data at any time.

Pro adds two things: your complete history, personal records and trends with no time limit, and the ability to import past workouts from a file. Pro costs $3.99 per month or $29 per year, in US dollars.
