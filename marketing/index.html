Questions worth asking

Do my kids need their own logins?

No. One login for the household — everyone else is a profile you tap to switch. Their data stays completely separate from yours.

Does it really work without signal?

Yes, for logging — sets, edits, notes, favourites, new exercises. Setup changes like routines, tags and adding people need a connection, and the app tells you so.

Is there an app to install?

Add it to your home screen and it runs full-screen like any other app, offline included. No app store, no updates to chase.

What happens to my older workouts on Free?

Nothing is deleted, ever. Free shows the last 90 days; Pro shows all of it. Subscribe and your full history is back instantly.

Pounds or kilos?

Either. Set a default for new sets; sets you've already logged keep the unit you recorded them in, and mixed histories are normalised before anything gets ranked.

Who is this actually for?

It was built for a dad and his sons sharing one iPad in a garage. If that sounds like your house, it will fit.
